3. Espera 1-2 min y comprueba:
   - https://losmorruos.github.io/OneSignalSDKWorker.js
   - https://losmorruos.github.io/OneSignalSDKUpdaterWorker.js
4. OneSignal → SITE URL: https://losmorruos.github.io → Save
